## Findings / notes
- **Greedy hang root cause (fixed):** stale snu55 `byte_trie.py` → greedy infinite-loop on BOS.
- **streaming-bt = batch-bt** for complete-answer scoring (parser contract verified).
- **FDW** (first-divergent-word probe): works for binary/distinct options (PIQA ~0.66), fails for
  shared-prefix options (HellaSwag ~floor). Greedy ≈ bt on FDW too (backtracking benign).
- **Byte-model few-shot caveat:** ~8192-byte budget can't hold many long shots (BoolQ, HellaSwag
  @10-shot) → lm-eval left-truncates → effective shots < nominal. aunet2/llama fit better.
