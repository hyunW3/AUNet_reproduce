SuperBPE level-2: does a learned coarse pool beat a fixed one?

Six 100M byte models, one variable — the level-2 pooling rule (learned SuperBPE superwords vs static 2-subword vs none/word). Each in two boundary regimes: offline (real-BPE, boundaries peek at the future) and causal (root_greedy, leak-free). BPB and full 6-bench downstream. Leak-free, the learned superword wins both.

Bits-per-byte (final)

Lower is better. Final-window training loss ÷ ln2 — a same-budget cross-model proxy (all models predict bytes, so directly comparable). Learned superword is best in both regimes; causal BPBs sit ~0.09 above offline (the leak the future-peeking offline boundaries hide).

BPB = mean(last-20 loss/out) ÷ ln2. Offline = bpe_br (non-causal, boundaries use future bytes). Causal = root_greedy (streaming-reproducible, leak-free). Reference in each group is the word baseline (offline) / 2-stage root_greedy (causal).

Downstream accuracy

0-shot, full test sets. all-6 = mean(HS, ARC-E, ARC-C, BoolQ, PIQA, WinoGrande); avg3 = mean(HS, ARC-E, PIQA). Offline → the word baseline leads; causal → the learned superword leads all-6 (BPB rank = downstream rank). But the causal all-6 gap is BoolQ-driven — on avg3 the ordering flips.

acc_norm for HS/ARC-E/ARC-C/PIQA, acc for BoolQ/WinoGrande. Plain apps.aunet.eval (offline models) / native online-3-valued eval path (causal models); train==eval boundaries verified.

Per-benchmark

Every task, both regimes. Bold = best in regime · italic = near-chance at 100M (ARC-C chance ≈ 25, WinoGrande ≈ 50) · amber = the root_greedy BoolQ collapse a level-2 repairs.

The swing task is BoolQ. The causal 2-stage reference drops to 41.3 (a known root_greedy weakness); adding any level-2 lifts it to 52–57. That single column is most of the causal all-6 story. HellaSwag is a ~30 near-tie everywhere.

Offline vs causal — the leak

Offline bpe_br decides boundaries from the whole text, so a boundary can depend on future bytes an autoregressive decoder never has — an optimistic ~0.09-BPB "leak". root_greedy commits left-to-right (leak-free), reproducible at generation. The two regimes disagree on the downstream winner; the leak-free result is the honest one.

Δ = causal − offline for the two shared families (superword, 2-subword). The word baseline (offline) and 2-stage ref (causal) are the respective "no learned level-2" references and aren't paired. Leak penalty is consistent (~+0.09 BPB) across arms — the offline advantage is future-peeking, not modeling.

Configuration

All six share the arch family and the AU-Net scaling-law recipe (global batch 48 · 53,504 steps · 21 GB · seq 8192). The only deliberate difference is the level-2 pooling rule; the 2-stage ref drops the level-2 stage.

llama3-derived SuperBPE tokenizer (128k→200k, 72,713 superwords over full llama3, 99.98% clean-nesting). Level-2 nests exactly inside level-1 (verified 100%). 2-stage ref ran micro 24 / grad-acc 2 (global 48 held) to fit the 80 GB A100.
