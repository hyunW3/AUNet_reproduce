## Why the split
v2_online (online `bt`) and v4_root_greedy (online `greedy@root`) **stall in the generate phase**
(online boundary prediction inside the autoregressive decode) and were starving the B200s. Offload
those evals to snu55 so the B200s stay free for training. v1_committed + v3_offline already done here.
